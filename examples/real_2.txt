NASA's Perseverance rover has successfully collected its twentieth rock sample
from the Jezero Crater on Mars, scientists confirmed Tuesday. The sample,
roughly the size of a piece of chalk, will be stored in a sealed tube on
the Martian surface for potential retrieval by a future mission. Researchers
believe the crater once held an ancient lake and may contain signs of past
microbial life. The samples are expected to reach Earth no earlier than 2033.
